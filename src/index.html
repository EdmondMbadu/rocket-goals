RocketGoals: Goal Setting, Habit Tracking, and AI Coaching

RocketGoals helps people turn goals into daily execution with a clear framework, focused routines, and measurable progress. The platform combines planning, habit tracking, accountability, and AI-assisted coaching to help users stay consistent.

Core methods include the ROCKET framework, Instant Opportunity OS, and Antifragility principles for building momentum under pressure. Users can define high-priority goals, break them into steps, track completion, and review performance over time.

What You Can Do in RocketGoals

  • Create structured goals with milestones and action plans.
  • Track daily habits and execution consistency.
  • Use AI guidance to prioritize next actions and reduce friction.
  • Review progress with a mission-style dashboard.
